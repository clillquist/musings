Its been years since I had my own blog or website!  Why, because when I built for companies I left my own druthers behind me and focused on the mission at hand.  A self reflection around that may come, but really it was a balance of working for others and then taking time to myself outside of the computer.

When I first made websites, it was a way to showcase some of the photography that I had produced, it was fun!  I want that to continue here too.

Since my last website publication, a lot has changed - mainly the advent use of AI.  Also as of mid 2026, I have been laid off and took some time away from the keyboard for a bit.  This may of been a questionable choice due to the fast paced changes that are occurring right now but I had time for travel and for my family.

What can you expect from me here?  I am going to use this as outlet to write ideas and expound thoughts.  I think as we move towards lightspeed, leaving a trail of thoughts that might help reflect in these times.

This is also a chance for a creative kickstart to a past passion, photography.  At times I will share photos or sets from activities or places that interest me. 

The last part is exploration of new technologies around the industry I take part in.  My take and understanding - right or wrong - its a way to understand the topic at hand.

Really this is just about me expressing myself, working the writing muscle, a creative outlet, and a way for me to understand the world as we move forward.
